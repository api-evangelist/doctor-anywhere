# Doctor Anywhere

> Doctor Anywhere is a Singapore-headquartered, tech-led regional healthcare company founded in 2015, serving more than 2.8 million users across Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, the Philippines and Indonesia with telehealth and in-person care. Doctor Anywhere does not operate a public developer portal and publishes no OpenAPI definition; its platform APIs run behind an Apigee gateway serving its own consumer and provider apps. Its regional marketing sites for Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia are built on Wix and each publish an llms.txt plus an anonymous Model Context Protocol endpoint over public site content.
